  Aaron Hendry is a Youth Development Worker, rangatahi advocate, writer, and

  #EndYouthHomelessness in Aotearoa. He is also a board member for Massey
  Community Trust and has had a key role in developing the Safety Net Project, a
  community initiative to support rangatahi experiencing homelessness.
