  **Short Term** _free boarding community_


  In partnership with Massey Community Trust, we've created a community of host
  homes who offer safe, temporary accommodation for rangatahi.  
    
  Host homes provide a safe temporary space for a young person, while they
  reconnect with whanau or make decisions about other housing options.  
    
  We match people in the community with the capacity to help provide temporary
  accommodation with those who need it.
